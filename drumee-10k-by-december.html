How I choose: cash per day of work, closest people first

Each bet gets one number: expected cash by 31 December divided by the days of work it takes. Sorted that way, the list follows how well the buyer already knows Drumee.

1 · already gave a card for $1about $1,050 a day
2 · already use Drumeeabout $350

5 · know the categoryabout $170
6 · friends of usersabout $170
7 · new to Drumeeabout $100
8 · a partner's customersabout $70
closest to Drumeeexpected cash by 31 Dec per day of work

I call this the path of least friction. For the first customers, start where switching costs the buyer least and saying yes takes the fewest steps. Land them there, then build the repeatable motion from what those deals teach us. The same rule runs inside each deal: find the route with the fewest approvals and a clear owner, or create one.

It also tells me what to stop when time runs short. If week two shows the base is smaller than I hope, the days move to the Node and the launch, in that order.

One check on my own time: the bets I own add up to about 45 days of work, and the window has about 30 working days. So growth builds the outbound list and the partner shortlist, and every kill rule hands its days to the bet with the best cash per day so far.
