4The system

Before

A 60-second brief by buyer type, including why now.

During

The card for who is on the line and what they just said.

After

The same short recap every time, plus a handoff card engineering gets before any technical call.

The bar

Every AE and SDR clears the drill and an engineer-scored live call before a solo first call.

Where call one comes from. The trigger we watch is a change in how an account gets paid: going monthly, opening a new country, a new filing rule. That tells an SDR who to call this week and what to open with.

The loop. Every "not on this call" a buyer pushes on goes to product on Friday. Every recap gap becomes a card.

5A glimpse, already built

The call-one kit

I built the first piece from outside, from our public docs, pricing and the founding team's posts. Pick a buyer, open what they said, copy the recap.

Building it turned up three things worth knowing:

12 of 16

12 of the 16 cards could be built from our public docs and pages. 4 need an answer only the founding team has. That is the job: getting those out of a few heads and into every seller's hands.

71.3%

Against a gateway that also charges 3%, the whole price gap is our fixed $0.35, and the answer is a number, not a discount. At a $20 ticket, if they convert 70% today, list price pays for itself at 71.3% success. The pilot has to beat 71.3%, not 95%.

4 lines

A new seller most needs the lines not to cross, where they will see them.

  • No Shopify or WooCommerce plugin and no native mobile SDK in our docs.
  • Platform Connect is switched on by us, per account.
  • Sandbox access comes through support, after account verification.
  • Our Payouts doc says payouts are made in USD, with a $500 default minimum.

Once those are on the card, nobody promises otherwise, and engineering never inherits a promise sales made.

The obvious question

I did not start as an engineer. The filter is there because a buyer's engineer can tell when a seller is guessing, and call one has to run without one of ours. Judge that on the kit: it is built from our docs, and it says idempotency keys, webhook signature checks and the 4, 8, 12 and 24 hour retry schedule for failed renewals as plainly as it says where the docs stop. At Simplifai we sold AI automation into banks and Upstox, starting from zero. And the bar applies to me before anyone: the drill, then a live call with one of our engineers as the buyer's engineer. If I guess, I don't go solo.

9Where I could be wrong

  • If the answers already live in a doc the team uses, this is distribution, not writing, and faster.
  • If most enterprise deals start from a warm founder intro, the Before brief matters more than the call card.
  • If compliance decides before finance at listed companies, the locked compliance card gets filled first.
  • If most new revenue arrives inbound, speed to lead beats triggers, and Why now becomes a routing rule.

Any of these would show inside the first month.
