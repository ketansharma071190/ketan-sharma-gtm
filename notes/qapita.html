    How the desk runs

    1. One record per Pulley conversation, written on the call, with the fields Pulley hands Carta at opt-in: stakeholders, plan, share classes, security types, subscription term.

    2. No Pulley deal closes as lost without the month they signed elsewhere. A loss to Carta is a dated opportunity in late 2027, not a dead row.

    3. Every row carries a next date. Monday's review covers only the rows due in the next 14 days, and each one leaves the meeting with a named owner.

    4. Prices come off the published US page, never improvised on a call. Year two always goes in writing.

    5. Every Pulley win is asked, once its first audit closes, to be the reference for September 2027.

    6. The health check runs before the proposal, not after the signature. A clean export is the proof that we can move them.
