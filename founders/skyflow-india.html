RBI has drafted one source of truth for every bank data element. Models included.

Anshu, on 15 July the Reserve Bank released draft guidance on data governance for eleven kinds of regulated entity, from commercial banks and NBFCs to credit bureaus. It asks for one authoritative source per data element, with every downstream system and model derived from it, and it names tokenization as a control. Comments closed on 17 August. As far as I can find, the final has not been issued, and my read is that when it is, the buyer for a vault inside an Indian bank stops being only the CISO. Below is how I would sell into that, beside your APAC team. Where I am guessing, I say so.
