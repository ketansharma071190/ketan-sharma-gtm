Banks fund inside the corporate's program, or bring us corporates.

We sell to corporates. Banks and partners work three ways around that sale, and each has its own motion.

  • Referral. Design the ask around what the RM is measured on: fee income, book growth, the SCF book. Multi-bank and own-cash discounting are what the platform adds to any single bank.
  • White-label. Lead with your live bank case once it can be named in India.
  • Timing. Bank onboarding takes six months to four years, so 2027 counts only bank deals already in motion. RBI's IT outsourcing directions mean heavy vendor due diligence.
  • Which banks. Private and foreign banks are the faster door. Public sector banks buy by tender with eligibility filters.
  • Economics in India. A few bps of disbursals, or a licence plus about 20% a year for support. Rivals in this lane: Veefin (Yes Bank, IndusInd, PSB Alliance) and CredAble (Karnataka Bank).
