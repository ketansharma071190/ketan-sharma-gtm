05 · Pilot design

30 suppliers, one entity, 90 days from clearance, with success signed before day one.

Small enough to start on the corporate's own cash, written so that the rollout is a scope change on the same paper.

FilledThresholds are defaults the CFO replaces

Scope: one of everything

One programOne legal entityOne plant or one categoryOne ERP link
Cohort 1 · Own cash20 suppliers

Micro, small and mid suppliers on the longest terms. On the corporate's own cash, so they start on day 1 of the 90.

Cohort 2 · Bank-funded, in parallel10 suppliers

Bigger suppliers funded by the bank that already runs the corporate's acceptances. They start when that bank's paper clears. Cohort 1 is already running by then.

30 suppliers in all, about Rs 1.7 crore of the corporate's cash rolling for cohort 1 on the brief's figures.

The evaluation plan, shown in the first meeting

Shown early so InfoSec and legal start in week one. The 90 days start when they clear, at Gate A. Setup at a large Indian corporate often runs 12 to 16 weeks, so the date is firmed with their IT team in week one, and promised from then.

First early payment: week 2 of the 90 days.

Two gates

Gate A · the start line, day 1
  • InfoSec cleared
  • Order form signed, fee from go-live
  • Tax route signed: GST on the discount
  • Supplier terms approved
  • Rate card and floor signed by treasury
  • Interface tested on one entity
  • Helpdesk staffed, 20 suppliers ready to invite
Gate B · end of week 6
  • At least 2 in 3 invited suppliers registered
  • At least 40% of those registered have taken one early payment
  • Every item reconciled
  • Bank cohort live, or a dated plan for it

The success sheet

Written and signed before day one. The CFO sets the numbers. The last line is in his own words, taken from the first meeting, and becomes the day-90 test.

  1. Every approved cohort invoice paid within 45 days.
  2. % of the cohort signed up by week .
  3. Yield on cash used above the treasury hurdle of %.
  4. Approval to payment in days or less for suppliers who take early pay.
  5. One report across every funder in the pilot.

Day 90: scale it, change it, or stop it

  • Commercial wrapper. A time-boxed pilot under an order form signed at Gate A, with the fee from go-live, so the rollout is a scope change. That signature is what the plan counts as signed. Whether pilots are free or paid is yours to set; this is the shape to confirm.
  • Rollout in waves. Then a referral asked at every go-live.
Next entity or plant→Top 20 to 30% of spend→The long tail→Dealers, same platform

Small starts grow, as the India electronics client did when it added discounting.

Walk away when

  • The pilot stays open-ended, with success criteria left blank.
  • The signer stays unnamed.
