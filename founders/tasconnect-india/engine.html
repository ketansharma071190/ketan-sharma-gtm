Screener

The unlisted ones. Lenovo India and Syngenta India file their accounts with the registry.
Rating rationales
CRISIL, ICRA and CARE: the rating that sets the bank rate on the page, and the banks named behind each facility.
TReDS
Which platforms it buys on, where announced, so the programme sits beside them and serves the suppliers outside TReDS.
News
New plants, capex, a new treasurer, a results call that mentions working capital.

2 · CRM: filled, then kept currentThe record, the committee, the stage
  • The account record carries the numbers above, so every seller starts from the same facts.
  • The committee map by desk: the CFO signs, treasury owns and co-signs, procurement runs the supplier waves, IT security clears. Who signs is the template.
  • A stage moves only on evidence: a named signer, success criteria in writing, an order form. The recap of each call files it.
  • The next step always has a date.
3 · Pages: one per company, one per readerThe same account, three rooms

The CFO gets the value case with his own figures pre-filled and editable. Treasury gets the bank view and where its cash beats the liquid fund. Procurement gets the supplier side. Each goes out the day of the meeting.
