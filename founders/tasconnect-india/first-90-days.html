The account engine, live by day 30

Built with Claude Code in the first month, so sellers spend their time in conversations and everything around a meeting is already done. It is what lets one person work about 40 accounts a quarter.

1 · Research

Every account read from its own filings

The supplier finance note, the MSMED table, cash against borrowings, payable days, dealer receivables, group links. Scored for tier and friction before anyone writes to it.

2 · CRM

Filled without typing

Contacts placed on the committee map by desk, stage evidence logged from each meeting, the next step dated.

3 · A page per account

Their numbers, editable, for each reader

The CFO gets the case on his figures, the treasurer the bank view, procurement the supplier waves. Sent the same day as the meeting, like the account and CFO pages in this playbook.

4 · The rhythm

Prep and reporting drafted for review

Call prep before every meeting, the recap after it, and the Monday one-pager from the CRM. A person checks each one before it goes.

What it reads: public filings and registry data, plus your CRM, inside your data policy and on tools your security team approves. Client data never goes into a model your security team has not cleared. It needs ask 20: a CRM seat, the call-recording consent policy, and a sandbox.

What it needs from the team

Each ask with what it unlocks. Mark each one; the two that change capacity show their effect below. Marks stay in this browser.

What comes back

  • The Monday one-pager, every week, by 9am Singapore time.
  • A playbook version every two weeks.
  • An honest status on every deal at day 30, 60 and 90.

People are added on pipeline triggers, never on the calendar: the AE from Q2, when the cold cohorts need more accounts worked than one person covers; presales support for four pilots at a time, rising to six in Q4.
