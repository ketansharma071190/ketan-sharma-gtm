Economic buyerCFO

Signs. Carries it to the audit committee if it is material.

Program owner, championTreasurer

Owns banks, limits and surplus. Co-signs.

  • The software contract also passes IT or vendor procurement, InfoSec and legal.
  • At an MNC subsidiary, regional or global treasury (often in Singapore) frequently holds the decision.
  • At promoter-led groups, anything touching banks or treasury policy gets a final nod from the MD or the promoter's office.
  • Bank-funded programs need each bank's credit sanction. If a program counts as borrowing, it may need a board resolution within the company's borrowing limit. The company secretary confirms.
  • Where the group has its own NBFC (Mahindra Finance, Hero FinCorp, Bajaj Auto Credit), its head is a funder to bring in, not a rival to route around.
  • Own-cash discounting pays the corporate's own approved invoices early. That is a payment decision inside treasury policy, with no new borrowing.

Rules for mapping it

  1. Map six core desks in the first meeting: CFO, treasury, procurement, plant head, IT, tax. Ask who owns each.
  2. No evaluation plan until the CFO, treasurer, IT and InfoSec are all engaged.
  3. Own cash takes the banks off the critical path, and tax gains weight (the GST route on the discount). Bank-funded makes the banks the slowest track: run it in parallel.
  4. At an MNC subsidiary, ask early: "Does regional treasury decide this, or do you?"
  5. Map the committee in person at head office. The real deciders in Indian finance teams are often not on LinkedIn.
