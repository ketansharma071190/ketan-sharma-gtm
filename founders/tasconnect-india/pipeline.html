A cohort is worked in the two quarters before it signs

So the Q1 and Q2 cohorts are already being worked today, by your team. The cold cohorts start on day one and carry Q3 and Q4.

Capacity: accounts set the first hire

First meetings stay at 12 to 24 a quarter, inside one person all year. Accounts worked grow faster: with the account engine doing research, pages and CRM, Ketan covers about 40 a quarter. From Q2, the accounts above 40 are the trigger for one AE, plus bank and partner referrals.

Scoped pilots in flight: 4 a quarter from Q1, 6 in Q4. That is the presales trigger.

Stages, and the evidence that moves a deal

  1. 0TargetTier, trigger, at least three roles named
  2. 1First meetingThe pain in their words
  3. 2QualifiedCFO or treasurer confirms an outcome, a route and a time frame
  4. 3Evaluation planAgreed in writing; InfoSec and legal packs sent
  5. 4Business caseOn their own data, owned by the treasurer
  6. 5PaperLegal, procurement and InfoSec cleared
  7. 6SignedCounts toward the plan's signed ACV
  8. 7LiveFirst early-paid or financed invoice
  9. 8ExpandingNext entity or wave named, referral asked

    Getting the second meeting

    • Leave something they need to correct: their own numbers on their own page.
    • Agree the next meeting and who comes to it, in the room.
    • Send the recap and the page the same day, and the written success sheet within three working days.
    • The second meeting is where they bring the right numbers and usually one more person. Ask: “Who else should see this before we meet again?”
