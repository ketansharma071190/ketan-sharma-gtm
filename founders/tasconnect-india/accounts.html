Facts
Rs 382 crore paid to micro and small suppliers after the due date in FY26 (Rs 499 crore in FY25), with about Rs 6.5 crore of interest accrued. Consolidated supplier finance: Rs 2,694.74 crore.
Who
Group CFO Amarjyoti Barua, and treasury. The committee map is loaded with M&M.

The program is growing fast across several finance providers. FY27 is year two of the Ind AS 7 note, so comparatives arrive and every bank's data is needed again. 31 Mar 2027 is the next 45-day year-end.
