MAS issued final guidelines on AI risk management for every financial institution in Singapore, and named agentic AI systems that operate on their own and access tools. Firms should identify their AI use and keep inventories, and run lifecycle controls: testing, human oversight, monitoring and change management. Third-party AI gets no pass: the bank stays accountable, and should test the service on its own use cases and data and keep that testing on file.

First 90 days

From four AEs to a region you can forecast

What I would do, and what you would see at the end of each month.

Inherit and inspect

  • One-on-ones with the four AEs. Walk every open deal and agree forecast categories with the Global VP of Sales.
  • Sit in on customer calls in Sydney and Singapore, and spend a day with the deployed engineers.
  • Build the account map: 40 named banks, insurers and large enterprises across Australia and Singapore, plus ten SIs.
  • Meet the AWS and Google Cloud partner teams in region, and three SI practice leads.
  • Draft the APAC page and a one-page MAS answer with your legal and security teams.

What you see by day 30

  • A forecast by AE you can believe, with the deals I would drop and why.
  • Three named lighthouse targets and the reasons.
  • The partner shortlist and an H1 hiring plan.

Lighthouses in writing

  • Agree pilots with two lighthouse accounts: written success criteria, a named signer, procurement and security in from week one.
  • Sign a joint plan with one global SI and one regional SI on named accounts.
  • Open the first two roles and run structured hiring with the talent team.
  • Weekly pipeline review and deal coaching become the team's rhythm.

What you see by day 60

  • Two pilots agreed in writing, or a clear no and the reason.
  • Partner-sourced pipeline tracked as its own line.
  • Candidates in final rounds.

First proof

  • Run the pilots against their goals, with a review every two weeks.
  • Write the first result up with the customer's sign-off, so the next bank will believe it.
  • First joint review with each partner.
  • The H1 plan for APAC: number, headcount and partner pipeline, by sub-region.

What you see by day 90

  • Early numbers from one regulated account, in writing.
  • Partner-sourced pipeline as a share of all APAC pipeline.
  • An H1 plan with each hire tied to the number.

A big bank will not sign a year's contract inside 90 days. The honest day-90 result is pilots running against written goals with named signers, and a forecast you can trust. That is how we did it at Simplifai.
