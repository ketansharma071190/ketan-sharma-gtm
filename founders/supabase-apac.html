First 90 days

One APAC plan, with India inside it

What I would do, and what you would see at the end of each month.

What I do

  • Sit with the ASEAN and ANZ AEs, the solution architect and the TAM. Agree who covers which accounts and what stays automated.
  • Pull every APAC account with its triggers and spend. Rank India's top 100.
  • Build the DPDP pack with your legal and product teams.
  • Meet 20 Indian customers on Pro and Team, most of them in person in Bengaluru, Mumbai and Pune.
  • Meet the AWS partner teams in Mumbai and Singapore, and the platform teams at Peak XV and Lightspeed.

What you see by day 30

  • An APAC coverage map on one page.
  • India's top 100 accounts, each with the trigger that put it there.
  • The DPDP pack, approved.
  • Notes from 20 customers, in their words.

What I do

  • Work India's triggered accounts directly: automated outreach first, a call when they reply.
  • Run a weekly pipeline review with the two AEs: stuck deals, partner deals, forecast.
  • Open one Indian builder conversation, with you on the first call.
  • Write the specs for the next two hires.

What you see by day 60

  • Pipeline by country and segment, in HubSpot.
  • India accounts that talked to us, tracked against your 25 and 125 percent.
  • The first Indian upgrades from Pro to Team.
  • Two job posts ready to go.

What I do

  • Take the first Indian Enterprise deal into procurement, with legal, security and finance in from week one.
  • Write the India playbook so the next hire starts from it.
  • Forecast APAC by country.
  • Write the 12-month APAC plan.

What you see by day 90

  • A forecast you can hold me to.
  • One Indian Enterprise deal in procurement, with a named signer.
  • The next hires in interviews.
  • A 12-month plan: revenue, cost, hiring.

Upgrades to Team can happen inside 90 days. An Indian Enterprise contract usually will not. So the day-90 test is a deal in procurement with a named signer, and India's upgrades measured against the rest of APAC.
