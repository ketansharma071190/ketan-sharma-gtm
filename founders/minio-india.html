A working draft for AB Periasamy · 24 November 2026

India just wrote an object store into its GPU tender. The sizing is still blank.

AB, on 20 November C-DAC issued the final RFE to empanel the clouds that will run up to 10,000 GPUs for the IndiaAI Mission. Every cluster must have access to an object store, and everything, control plane included, must stay in India. Bids close on 4 December. How much storage, and how fast, is left to Stage 2. Below is how I would sell MinIO into that, and into the rest of India. Where I am guessing, I say so.

C-DAC/GPU/01/2026 · what every cluster must havefinal RFE, 20 Nov 2026
Compute

Up to 10,000 AI compute units (GPUs or equivalent) in clusters of 4,000, 1,000 or 512, for five years, for ministries, academia, startups and industry.

Network

A networking layer for training and inference, with per-tenant micro-segmentation.

Storage, 4.1(1)(c)

Cluster shall have access to the parallel file systems and object store for training and inferencing AI workload management. It should scale linearly as nodes are added.

Sovereignty, 4.5

Data plane and control plane, data, metadata, logs, backups and DR remain within the sovereign territory of India.

What that tells me

  1. The RFE names two storage layers. The parallel file system has a known India incumbent. The object store has none I can see, and the clause is written so it scales with every node added.
  2. The sovereignty clause puts the control plane in India too. Self-hosted software clears that; a storage service run from abroad has to explain itself. my read
  3. India has field engineers and a seller coming, and, as far as I can see from outside, nobody who owns the number, the partners or the government calendar. Riyadh shows what a sovereign program looks like when someone does.
  4. The bidders are few and named. This is an account plan of about ten logos, not a market to blanket.
