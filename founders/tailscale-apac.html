  1. The first APAC pipeline is already inside APAC companies. Free and Standard tailnets on company domains in Singapore, Sydney, Bengaluru and Tokyo. Working those comes before cold outbound. guess on how many
  2. Two reasons to buy, two motions. Replacing the VPN box after a breach or a forced change sells to the mid-market, through partners. Fortinet removed SSL VPN tunnel mode in FortiOS 7.6.3, and old tunnel settings do not carry over, so every FortiGate shop still on it has to move its remote access before it upgrades. First 90 days

Turn usage into a region

What I would do, and what you would see at the end of each month.

What I do

  • Pull APAC usage: paying and company-domain tailnets by country and size.
  • Talk to 20 APAC users and customers, Mercari too if you allow it.
  • Sit in on HAT's live deals in Australia.
  • Meet 10 partners in Singapore and India.
  • Write down how the APAC deals so far were won or lost.

What you see by day 30

  • One page: where APAC revenue and usage sit, and the two segments and two countries I would start with, with numbers.
  • 15 named opportunities from existing usage.
  • Three partners in SEA and three in India, with reasons.
  • Hiring plan, first version.

What I do

  • Work the existing tailnets with the SE: who owns it, what they need to scale it.
  • Start outbound to the 20 example accounts.
  • Sign the first SEA and India partners on your channel agreement, and train them on one migration playbook.
  • First hire starts.
  • Weekly forecast in the same format as North America.

What you see by day 60

  • Pipeline by country and segment, staged the way you stage it at home.
  • The first partner-registered deal.
  • Win and loss notes in buyers' own words.

What I do

  • Close the first commercial deals from pipeline APAC created.
  • Run one workshop with a partner in Singapore for their customers.
  • Open the Japan distributor conversation.
  • Write the 12-month APAC plan: target, hires, cost, and when APAC pays its way.

What you see by day 90

  • First closed revenue from APAC-created pipeline.
  • A partner-sourced deal in stage.
  • The 12-month plan, and a call on which country gets the next seller.

Large enterprise deals will not close inside 90 days. The day-90 proof is closed commercial deals, a staged pipeline you can forecast from, and partners who can sell without me in the room.
