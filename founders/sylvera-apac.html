For Allister Furey · Sylvera · draft, 27 Nov 2026

On 26 October India posted a draft of how its compliance credits would trade. As drafted, there is no secondary market in it.

The Bureau of Energy Efficiency posted its draft Detailed Procedure for Transaction of Carbon Credit Certificates (dated October 2026) for public comment on the Indian Carbon Market portal on 26 Oct 2026, the upload date on the portal file. It proposes how certificates under the Carbon Credit Trading Scheme would move between companies, and as drafted it closes almost every route that normally produces price data. BEE draft procedure, PDF ICM portal notice

BEE draft procedure, Oct 2026under the CERC CCC Regulations of 27 Feb 2026
ClauseWhat the draft saysWhat it does to price data
  • 4.3
    What the draft saysBilateral trading even within the same corporate group is not permitted.
    What it does to priceNo OTC deals, so no broker quotes and no negotiated prices to collect.
  • 14.5
    What the draft saysPower exchanges discover one market clearing price per trading session through a closed double-sided uniform price auction.
    What it does to priceOne print per session per exchange, not a continuous market.
  • 14.4(c)
    What the draft saysCertificates bought on an exchange shall not be eligible for resale under any circumstances.
    What it does to priceNo secondary trading, no forward curve, no traders to make one.
  • 14.4(c)
    What the draft saysA Non-Obligated Entity, a voluntary buyer, may buy only for retirement, with no resale or banking.
    What it does to priceIndian companies would get a domestic, retire-only credit to weigh against voluntary credits.
  • 15.3(h)
    What the draft saysAfter each session, exchanges display demand and supply curves on their websites.
    What it does to priceThe raw data is public. The read on it is not.
Who this binds: about 490 obligated entities in seven sectors had targets by February 2026, ICAP, and a revised draft notification of June 2026 would add 255 iron and steel units, Down To Earth. The procedure is a draft out for comment, so clauses may still change. The exchanges listed on the portal are IEX, HPX and PXIL.

What it means for Sylvera

A finance head at an Indian cement or aluminium plant would, under this draft, decide whether to cut emissions, buy certificates or bank them against a price that appears once per session and can never be resold. That is a forecasting problem with very little market data, which is the problem your Market Intelligence product is built for in the voluntary market: price data, policy and country risk, and forecasts. And every Indian company with a voluntary climate claim would get a retire-only domestic credit to compare with the Indian project credits you already rate. I could not find CCTS coverage on your public pages GUESS, so this may be a product question before it is a sales one. Either way, it is a reason to be in the room in India now.
