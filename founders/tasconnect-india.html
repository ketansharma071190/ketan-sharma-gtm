02Why nowThree rules put supplier payments on the CFO's desk.
  1. FY24Section 43B(h). Pay small suppliers late and the tax deduction waits until you pay.
  2. Mar 2025TReDS onboarding mandatory above Rs 250 crore turnover.
  3. FY26Ind AS 7. Supplier finance now disclosed in the accounts.
03The first meetingOpen on their number. Map who decides.
  1. BeforeRead four notes in their annual report: payables, MSME dues, supplier finance, cash. Pick one number. Build their page.
  2. OpenTheir number, then a question. For M&M: “Rs 1,370 crore to Rs 2,239 crore in a year. Who decides which supplier goes to which bank?”
  3. MapSix desks decide: CFO, treasury, procurement, plant head, IT, tax. Ask who owns each.
04ObjectionsSix we will hear, each answered in a line.
  • “TReDS already exists.”TReDS serves MSME sellers. This is your own program: every supplier, your banks, your cash.
  • “Our surplus is going into expansion.”The same invoices move to bank funding. Suppliers still get paid early.
  • “A new bank needs board approval.”Start on your own cash. No new bank, no new borrowing.
  • “Four people can do this by hand.”They can approve invoices. They can't make banks compete to fund them.
  • “Our plant head won't push suppliers.”He picks the pilot suppliers. Early pay stays the supplier's choice.
  • “Standard Chartered owns you.”You choose which banks see your workspace.
