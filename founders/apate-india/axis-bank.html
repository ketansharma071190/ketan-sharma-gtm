How the account gets named

  1. 1An Axis customer reports a scammer's number, WhatsApp ID or UPI handle.
  2. 2Apate's agents pick up the conversation and keep it going until the scammer names where to pay.
  3. 3Next morning the list reaches Financial Crime Intelligence, with every account named, at Axis and at other banks.

You send the scammer details your customers report, one file a day. Customer records stay inside Axis. What comes back is the accounts the scammers named.
