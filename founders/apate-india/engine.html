One bank, one week

Research and the pipeline run every Monday; the rest run around each meeting. People take the meeting and send every word. Tap a step to see what it reads and what it makes.

  1. 1ResearchEvery bank, every Monday, from public sources.
    Reads
    RBI's annual fraud tables. The fraud note in the bank's own annual report. I4C and police counts of mule accounts. Ombudsman, court and FIU orders that name the bank. Leadership changes. Tenders.
    Keeps
    A dated fact sheet for each bank. Every figure keeps its source in the file, and the page carries the number.
    Flags
    A new CRO, a new order or a new FIR moves the bank up that week's list.
    Axis, filed
    FY26 fraud note: 4,098 frauds. The February order: 5%. A Mumbai police FIR in July. A new CFO from 28 September.
  2. 2The CRMFilled from the buyer map, five seats a bank.
    Seats
    The CRO opens the door. The fraud head buys. Compliance backs it. The CISO clears it. The CFO sets the year's envelope.
    Each seat
    Name, in post since, last role, and what they have said in public.
    Each call
    Logged the day it happens, with the next step and its date.
  3. 3A page per readerOne per bank and per seat, with numbers they can change.
    CRO
    Liability from 1 January, the receiving-bank share, and the bank's own exposure.
    Fraud head
    Mule-account capture at that bank, and the four pilot numbers.
    CISO
    The security pack, mapped to RBI's outsourcing rules.
    CFO
    The FY28 line, sized on the bank's own numbers.
    See one
    Axis, as the CRO sees it. Axis, as the fraud head sees it.
  4. 4Meeting prepReady the night before.
    In the room
    Who, in post since when, and what each has said in public.
    Changed
    What moved since the last meeting, at the bank and in the rules.
    Brings
    Three questions and one ask.
  5. The India lead takes the meetingDali or Sam joins the first CRO meeting at each bank.
  6. 5Follow-upWithin the hour.
    Does
    Notes into the CRM, the follow-up drafted, the next date set.
    Sends
    Through the India lead, who reads every message before it goes.
  7. 6The Monday pipelineTo Dali and Sam, every Monday.
    Shows
    Every bank by stage: first meeting, scoping and security, pilot, signed.
    Calls out
    What moved, what is stuck, the next step and who owns it.
    Counts
    Only signed contracts count as won.
  8. 7Pilot weeksWeekly hit report and readout drafted, counts only.
    Reads
    The counts the bank shares against the four numbers.
    Drafts
    The one-page hit report every week, and the readout against the signed bars.
    Sends
