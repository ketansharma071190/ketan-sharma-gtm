The first bank, on the calendar

The warm bank is already trialling, so its pilot can be signed in December and its lists can start on the first Monday of January. Its contract papers are agreed while the lists run, so the readout only triggers the signature.

Sydney's summer break. Everything Sydney builds is done by 18 December. From 21 December to 8 January the agents and the daily list run without Sydney staff, and the India lead watches them.

What could slip, and the fix

RiskWhat we do
Too few scam conversations in the first weekThe pilot bank passes on the scammer numbers, WhatsApp IDs and UPI handles its customers report, from the day it signs. Apate's seeded numbers add volume. Mobileum's telco route comes later.
Few hits sit at the pilot bankAbout 1 in 75 captured accounts sits at any one large bank. Capture about 35 new accounts a day, set the bars as rates, and count accounts at other banks, reported through the national portal, as the fourth number.
Sydney's break, 21 Dec to 8 JanEverything Sydney builds is done by 18 Dec. The agents and the list run without Sydney staff, and the India lead watches them.
Security review runs longStart it in week 1, beside scoping. The pack answers most of it before the first meeting.
The warm bank wants the pilot freeCredit the fee to year one.
Indian numbers held up by DoT's KYC rulesGo through a licensed CPaaS or telco, starting in week 1.
Personas miss how Indian scams talkTest them with the warm bank's fraud team on real scripts in week 2.
The signature slips past 31 MarchThe papers are agreed during the pilot. If it still slips, the FY28 budget ask is in, so it signs in April from the new year's money.
RBI's mule rules change after 30 NovemberThe pilot does not depend on them. It gets stronger if they land.
