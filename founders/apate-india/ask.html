Product and setup

  • An India-region cloud tenancy, with logs kept in India for 180 days.
  • Personas in Hindi and Hinglish on Indian scam scripts, then the main regional languages.

  • A security pack mapped to RBI's 2025 outsourcing paras, and a one-page DPDP note.
  • A written legal opinion on diverting and recording scam calls, before any telco pilot.

Three decisions for the first week

  1. The pilot price, and whether the fee credits to year one.

    The warm bank needs this number by 11 December, to sign by the 18th.

  2. How India is billed.

    An invoice from Australia under reverse-charge GST, or a local entity. The warm bank's procurement asks before it signs.

  3. The first partner.

    Clari5 for reach into banks, or Mobileum for the telco route. Signed by 5 February.
