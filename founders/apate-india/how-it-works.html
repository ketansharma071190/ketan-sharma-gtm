Where Apate acts

Apate gets the mule account before the money moves.

India's tools are good at freezing and tracing. Each one learns about a mule account after money reaches it, or after a victim complains.

A digital arrest, step by step

The scam the new rules name. This is the common pattern in police and court filings, with who acts at each step today.

  1. 1 Day 0

    The hook

    A call from a "courier", then a "police officer": a parcel in the victim's name holds drugs.

    Today
    Telco spam filters, and TRAI's new AI flagging. They block or warn on numbers already seen.
  2. 2 Hours to days

    The conversation

    A video call with a fake officer. The victim is told they are under "digital arrest" and must tell no one.

    Today
    Nobody.
    Apate
    An agent plays the victim and keeps the scammer talking.
  3. 3 Before any payment

    Where to pay

    The scammer names a "verification" account, a UPI ID or a wallet.

    Today
    Nobody.
    Apate
    Captures the account and IFSC, UPI ID, phone numbers and wallet, with the time first seen.
  4. The money movesIndia's mule-account tools start here.
  5. 4 Seconds

    The payment

    The victim pays from their own bank, with their own PIN or OTP.

    Today
    The bank's checks pass. DoT's Financial Fraud Risk Indicator scores phone numbers already reported.
  6. 5 Minutes

    The money lands

    It reaches a mule account and moves on through more accounts.

    Today
    MuleHunter.AI, in 29 banks, and the bank's own early warning watch the receiving account.
  7. 6 Days later

    The complaint

    The victim calls 1930 or files on the national cybercrime portal.

    Today
    I4C's Suspect Registry logs the account, and the bank reports the fraud to RBI's registries. A hold, rarely a refund: 0.4% came back.

Where the scam conversations come from

Apate needs Indian scammers talking from the first day of a pilot, with no telco diversion. Three sources, in this order.

  1. Day 1

    The pilot bank's own reports

    Each day the bank's fraud team passes Apate the scammer numbers, WhatsApp IDs and UPI handles its customers report. These are the scammer's details, never the customer's. Apate's agents contact each one as a new victim.

  2. Day 1

    Apate's own seeded numbers

    Indian numbers Apate holds and seeds where scammers find their targets. They add volume beyond the bank's own customers.

  3. Later

    The telco route, through Mobileum

    Diverted scam calls at network scale, once a written legal opinion on diverting and recording is in place.

Three ways in, in this order

  1. Now

    Direct to one bank

    A daily list to the fraud team. No integration, no bank data in. This is the pilot.

  2. From month 2

    Through a fraud hub

    Clari5, part of Perfios, runs fraud monitoring at many of India's largest banks, and its Intelligence Hub already pulls in I4C's Suspect Registry. One integration reaches many banks.

  3. From month 3

    National rails

    RBI Innovation Hub runs MuleHunter.AI in 29 banks and a 7-bank DPIP pilot, and takes I4C's Suspect Registry under a May 2026 agreement. IDPIC has a pilot with 8 banks. NPCI and DoT each run their own fraud feeds. Slow, little cash, a lot of trust.
