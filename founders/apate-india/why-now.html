Why now

The bill for a scam is moving to the bank that received the money.

Between February and October 2026 an Ombudsman, RBI and the Supreme Court all moved the same way. In January the rules take effect, and in March the budgets close.

The numbers

Cyber fraud reported to the Home Ministry over three financial years, April 2023 to March 2026. One crore is 10 million rupees. Dollars at Rs 93.5.

  • Rs 56,087 crreported lostabout US$6.0bn
  • Rs 9,079 crfrozen in the receiving accounts, 16%about US$1bn
  • Rs 206 crcame back to victims, 0.4%about US$22m

The same release counts Rs 11,158 cr saved since 2021. Saved there means held in the receiving account.

The liability clock

Each step, and what it changes for the bank that holds the mule account.

  1. 25 Feb 2026

    The Ombudsman bills five receiving banks

    A Rs 22.92 cr digital-arrest case. Five receiving banks, all private, were told to compensate the victim, Rs 1.31 cr between them. Yes Bank drew the top rate, 7.5% of what reached it, for lapses on dormant and mule accounts. Axis, City Union, ICICI and IndusInd were told to pay 5% each. The victim's own bank was cleared.

    The receiving bank is now the one answering for the scam.
  2. 9 Apr 2026

    RBI: the victim now makes the payment

    RBI's discussion paper says account takeover frauds are now negligible. Most frauds are authorised push payments. The victim types their own PIN or OTP, so the payment passes the bank's checks.

    The early signal sits on the other side: the account the scammer asks for.
  3. 24 Jun 2026

    RBI issues the customer-liability rules

    Final directions on limiting customer liability in digital transactions, in force from 1 January 2027.

  4. 31 Jul 2026

    RBI's new fraud rulebook, in force the same day

    Every commercial bank needs a dedicated Data Analytics and MI unit, and early warning on non-credit transactions in near real time, watching "specifically" for money mule accounts. A board committee, the SCBMF, now oversees how well fraud risk management works. An official of at least General Manager rank owns fraud monitoring.

    Banks now have to show their own board what they catch early.
  5. 11 Sep 2026

    Draft rule: hold a suspected mule payment for up to 60 days

    RBI's draft KYC amendment proposes a temporary debit hold on suspected mule transactions of Rs 1,000 or more.

    A hold needs grounds. A scammer naming the account as the place to pay is strong grounds.
  6. 18 Sep 2026

    TRAI makes telcos flag spam with AI

    Each flag goes to the sender's operator within 2 hours, phased in over 90 days.

    The telcos watch the hook. The account stays with the bank.
  7. 29 Oct 2026

    Supreme Court tells RBI: at least the full loss

    RBI's rules must make a bank pay compensation of at least the amount lost where its due diligence failed, with strict liability on the bank and the victim made whole. RBI's KYC amendment on mule accounts is to be notified by 30 November and in force by 31 December. The Chief Justice asked how banks could use AI.

    Our read: a Rs 1.31 cr bill on a Rs 22.92 cr case could become the full Rs 22.92 cr.
  8. 1 Jan 2027

    The customer-liability rules apply

    A payment the customer was coerced into counts as fraudulent, and the bank carries the burden of proof. For one year a loss up to Rs 50,000 gets 85% back, capped at Rs 25,000, once. Below Rs 29,412, RBI pays 65%, the customer's bank 10% and the receiving bank 10%.

    Small cases cost the receiving bank at most Rs 2,941 each. The large digital-arrest cases are where the court's line would bite.
  9. Jan to Mar 2027

    Banks set FY28 budgets

    India's financial year runs April to March. Budgets for April 2027 onward are set in these three months.

  10. May 2027

    Full data protection duties apply

    India's DPDP Act obligations come in fully.

    Data stays a gate. So in the pilot, customer records stay inside the bank.

The budget window

A bank can fund a pilot from a fraud head's own budget. An annual contract needs a line in the year's budget, and the line is written between January and March.

  • Pilot signed by 31 March 2027: Apate is in the FY28 budget, with money from April 2027.
  • Start at the end of 2027: the next budget set is FY29, with money from April 2028.

What this means for Apate

  • The receiving bank has a new bill and a new duty to its board.
  • The signal it needs sits before the payment, with the scammer.
  • The window to get into FY28 closes on 31 March 2027.
