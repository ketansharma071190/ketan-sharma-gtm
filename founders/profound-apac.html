What that tells me

  1. Singapore can follow the London path: a founding team, events first, then field. London grew to about 75 people in roughly a year. my read
  2. Agencies carry more of the load in Asia. Regional brands buy search and media through networks, and those networks already sell AI answers. Two of your eight Singapore roles are agency roles, so I think you see it too. guess on the share
  3. Enterprise-only pricing makes APAC a named-account game from day one. Thirty brands and ten agencies is a list one GM can know personally.
  4. Answers change by country. In Sydney, Princess Polly's Destiny Flaherty made the point that one prompt gives different answers in the US, the UK and Australia. Local prompt sets are the first product ask.
  5. India runs well from Singapore. The same regional teams and agency networks often cover it, so India can start as a list and a test, with no office. India my read

First 90 days

From a list to signed logos

  • Get certified through Profound University and sit with the New York team moving to Singapore.
  • Name the 30 brands and 10 agencies, each with an owner and a next step.
  • Hold 25 first meetings, starting with the Zero Click Sydney and Singapore guests.
  • Agree the agency model with your partnerships team: who holds the licence, who resells.
  • Close the first two hires from the roles already posted.
  • Ten calls with Indian brands already buying ChatGPT ads, and with WPP and Omnicom India. India

  • Two offers accepted.
  • A one-page India read: go, wait, or no. India

What I do

  • Proposals to 8 brands, each built on that brand's own answer-engine picture.
