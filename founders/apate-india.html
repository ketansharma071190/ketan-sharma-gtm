  2. 29 Oct 2026Supreme Court: a bank that fails due diligence must repay the full loss.

    RBI's mule-hold rules are due by 31 Dec. Its draft allows a 60-day debit hold.

  3. 1 Jan 2027Banks repay scam victims. The receiving bank shares the bill.

    Victims get 85% back, up to Rs 25,000. Under Rs 29,412 the split is RBI 65%, the customer's bank 10%, the receiving bank 10%. The bank carries the burden of proof.
