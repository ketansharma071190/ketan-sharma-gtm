Apate in India: the plan · for Dali Kaafar · 25 Nov 2026

India is making banks pay for mule accounts. Apate finds them before the money moves.

Dali, my first note to you in October was about India's 1 January rule: from then, a payment a customer is coerced into counts as fraud, and the bank that received the money pays a share of the refund. On Friday you asked how I'd open India for Apate and what it would take, so here it is, with the numbers behind it.

The plan in five lines

  1. Turn the warmest bank from your first conversations with Indian banks into a pilot with written success criteria and a named signer, and make it India's first reference.
  2. Pair it with one telco, Airtel, so India has both halves Apate already has in Australia, a telco and a bank: Airtel diverts scam calls to the agents, and the pilot bank acts on what they find.
  3. Go next to the banks India has already ordered to pay for mule accounts: Yes Bank, IndusInd, Axis and ICICI.
  4. Answer where the data sits on page one of every proposal, and start procurement and security in week one, not after the pilot.
  5. Run India from India, inside Sam's APAC team. Singapore keeps its accounts.

Why Apate wins there

India's tools find a mule account after money reaches it

India's tools are good at freezing and tracing. Every one of them learns about a mule account from money that already moved or a complaint already filed. Apate is in the conversation.

Where each tool acts in an Indian scammy map, from public sources
  1. The hook call, SMS, WhatsAppTelco spam filters, TRAI's new AI flagging, Truecaller. They block or warn.
  2. The conversation minutes to daysApate. An agent plays the victim and keeps the scammer busy.
  3. Where to pay account, UPI ID, walletApate. The scammer hands over the details before anyone pays.
  4. The paymentBank checks pass, because the victim approves it with their own PIN or OTP. Airtel's OTP alert, DoT's risk score on reported phone numbers and behaviour tools such as BioCatch may catch a coached session.
  5. The money landsMuleHunter.AI and the bank's own monitoring watch the receiving account.
  6. The complaintThe 1930 helpline, the national portal, I4C's Suspect Registry. A hold, and rarely a refund.
Steps 2 and 3 are the only ones that surface the receiving account before the money moves. I found nobody in India working there today.

Before the money moves

Of Rs 56,087 crore reported, Rs 206 crore came back. The Department of Telecom says its fraud risk indicator had prevented Rs 5,043.73 crore of suspected losses between its May 2025 launch and August 2026, but it scores phone numbers already reported. Apate gets the account, UPI ID or wallet from the scammer before a real victim pays into it.

PIB · BW Businessworld, DoT's own figure

It feeds what banks already run

RBI's July directions tell banks to watch mule accounts specifically, in or near real time. Apate's feed is the outside signal for exactly that. It goes into a bank's own engine or a fraud hub as one more input, so nobody has to rip anything out.

RBI directions, paras 23 to 25

It fits the new liability

The receiving bank pays a share from January, the Ombudsman is already ordering it, and the Supreme Court has talked about strict liability. The proposed debit hold needs grounds to suspect an account. An account the scammer gave Apate's agent as the place to send money is about as good as grounds get. my read
