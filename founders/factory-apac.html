On 4 November India wrote an audit checklist for AI agents. Droids already cover most of it.

CERT-In, India's national cyber agency, published its AI Security Auditing Guidelines v1.0 dated 4 Nov 2026. Section 9.7 is about agentic AI, and it reads like a spec sheet for what an Indian bank's auditor can now ask of Droids. CERT-In, PDF, p.66

The weight is in the tiering. At Tier 2 the guidelines call for an independent audit by a CERT-In empanelled auditor at least annually, with periodic or continuous independent audit at Tiers 3 and 4, and an agent that "initiates consequential or semi-autonomous actions" scores High on autonomy. CERT-In, Table 11, pp.24 to 25 It is a guideline with no stated start date, not a statute, as TeamLease RegTech notes. But it gives India's auditors a checklist to work from.

What it means for Factory

Indian buyers of coding agents just got a national audit checklist, and the framework lines up with ISO/IEC 42001, which Factory already holds. The vendor that walks into a bank's risk committee with section 9.7 already answered, control by control, can shorten its security review. From public sources I could not see who carries that file into Indian banks, insurers and engineering centres for Factory. That is the job I would want to take on, beside your APAC team.
